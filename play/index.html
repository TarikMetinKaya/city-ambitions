Browser demo

Your first day, in the rain.

This is the opening of City Ambitions, rebuilt for the browser with the same prices and the same shoppers as the iPhone game. It takes about a minute.

The demo saves nothing and sends nothing. Your real company starts in the app.

What happens next in the game

On the second morning you lease your first store, pick what to sell, and hire two people. A coach walks you through the first week. Then the city opens up.
