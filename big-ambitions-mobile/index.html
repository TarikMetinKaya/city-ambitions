City Ambitions: built for the phone from the start

City Ambitions is a different game, made by a different, independent developer. It shares the fantasy: start with almost nothing in New York, open a shop, and grow it into a company. It drops what does not work on a phone, like walking and driving, and keeps the decisions.

Questions

Is Big Ambitions on iPhone or iOS?

No. Big Ambitions is available on PC through Steam. As of November 2026 there is no official iPhone or iPad version.

Is Big Ambitions on Android?

No. There is no official Android version of Big Ambitions as of November 2026.

Will Big Ambitions come to mobile?

Its developer, Hovgaard Games, has not announced a mobile version. The studio's announced plans are more updates and a first DLC for the PC game.

What is a game like Big Ambitions for iPhone?

City Ambitions is a business simulator for iPhone set in New York: you open a shop, set prices, hire staff, import stock and grow into a company. It is a different game by an independent developer, built for short sessions on a phone.

Is City Ambitions free?

The download costs nothing and your first shop has no time limit. One in-app purchase unlocks the full game.
