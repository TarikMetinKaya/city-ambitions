Updates and plans

What's new, and what's next

What the latest version added, what we are building now, and what is on the list after that.

Out now

Version 1.2: the city grows

Released 17 November 2026

Open a supermarket

Big baskets, thin margins. Milk, bread and fruit spoil after a few days, so fridges and order sizes matter.

Fast food rush hours

Lunch and dinner rushes bring the crowds. Hire cooks and upgrade the kitchen or watch the queue walk out.

A factory that runs on recipes

Buy raw materials, run machines and decide for every product: make it yourself or buy it wholesale.

Four or more products everywhere

Every kind of store sells at least four lines, and branches carry the whole range.

Container imports

Choose the source, the box size and how to pay: up front, by letter of credit or on landing.

Delivery contracts

Sign a supplier to top up your shelves every morning and stop forgetting orders.

Three delivery vehicles

A panel van, a city minibus or a box truck: trade capacity against speed on every route.

Luxury life and bank loans

Spend your fortune on watches, cars, an apartment, a yacht or a jet, or borrow to grow faster.

Also in 1.2

  • Four new languages: Japanese, Korean, Italian and Dutch. Ten in total.
  • Messages from the developer: write a note in the game and get an answer inside the game.
  • Supporter Pack: gold storefront signs and a supporter badge, for players who want to back the game.

In the works

Version 1.3: a reason to come back

The next update is about what happens between sessions: something new waiting for you each day, with no timers that block you from playing.

Client orders

Each day two clients make an offer. Pick one, ship it from your stock and get paid the next day.

Morning brief

When you come back, a short brief shows what happened while you were away and the one decision that needs you.

This week

A weekly ledger with a few goals that fit your company. Miss a day and nothing is lost.

Seven-day calendar

A daily reward of cash or a boost, with a bigger one on day seven.

After that

On the list

Scenario packs

New games with their own rules, such as a 90-day sprint or a start with no bank loans.

Android

An Android version is in early testing. iPhone comes first, so there is no date yet.

Ideas we are exploring

Deeper staff management, and a casino to run. These are ideas, not promises.

Plans change. Nothing on this page is a promise or a date; it is what we are working toward.
