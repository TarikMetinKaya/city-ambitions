City Ambitions Support

City Ambitions is a premium single-player iPhone business simulation. You inherit a family shop, its debt, and 180 days to build a stronger company.

Reset progress

Open the in-game Settings screen, choose Reset Progress, and confirm. This permanently replaces the save stored on your device.

Report a bug

Email tmkdevcomp@gmail.com. Include the app version shown on the App Store page, your iPhone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps that reproduce it. A screenshot or screen recording helps. Do not send personal information.

FAQ

What does the Full Game unlock?
The first 8 days are free. The Full Game is a one-time purchase that lets the clock run past Day 8, with every storefront, route, container, rival and ending. If you reinstall or change iPhone, open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase.
Does time continue when I close the game?
Only businesses covered by Executive Autopilot progress while you are away.
What is the goal?
Build the company and address the $100,000 family debt by Day 180. The company can continue afterward.
Why did sales stop?
Check Store Pulse for demand, stock, staff, checkout capacity, price, and opening hours.
When do orders arrive?
Local wholesale orders arrive the next morning. Other suppliers and containers take longer.
What are regulars?
Customers earned through reliable service. They can return and become a demand source you built.
Why rent storage?
Storage holds overflow stock and supplies shelves each morning. Rent is charged every day.
How do delivery routes work?
A van and driver move stock from storage to branches each morning, up to their capacity and stop limit.
What can happen to a container?
Source, size, payment, travel time, customs, delays, and damage can change its cost and arrival.
