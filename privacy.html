Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. What City Ambitions and cityambitions.com collect, and why.

City Ambitions is published by TMK Tech (Tarik Metin Kaya). This policy covers the iPhone game and this website.

The game

Your saved game

Your company and progress are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes that save.

Analytics

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to count a small set of gameplay events (for example finishing the opening, a daily close, or placing an order) with small numeric details such as the game day. Firebase also records basic device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, country, and an app instance identifier). We use this only to understand where players get stuck and to improve the game. See how Google handles Firebase data. From version 1.2 the game also sends crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): the state of the app when it crashed and the same device information.

Tracking and advertising identifier

iOS asks whether City Ambitions may track you. If you allow it, Firebase may read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so we can measure which of our own ads brought players to the game. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is never read. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

The Full Game is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is verified through Apple and through RevenueCat, a purchase service that receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

With your permission the game schedules local notifications on your device, for example a reminder that your shop is waiting. They are created on the device and no notification data is sent to us.

Feedback you write

If you choose to send us a message from the game, we receive the text you typed together with the game day, the moment it was asked, the app version, your device model, and your language and region. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), is not linked to your identity or advertising identifier, and is read only to fix problems and improve the game. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

What the game does not collect

No accounts, names, email addresses, contacts, photos, or location. We do not sell data.

This website

cityambitions.com uses Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) to count visits and clicks: pages viewed, the steps and result of the browser demo, taps on the App Store button, language changes and opened questions. Google receives your IP address (shortened for visitors in the EU), device and browser type, approximate location, the page you came from and a random cookie ID. Visitors in Europe are asked first and the script only loads after they allow it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); elsewhere it loads by default. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. The data is kept for at most 14 months, is not used for advertising and is not sold. Fonts and images are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes the technical data every web request carries, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

The browser demo runs in your browser; its steps are counted only as described above. Your browser's local storage remembers your cookie choice and whether you dismissed the language suggestion; that never leaves your device.

Daily leaderboard

If you choose to add your score to the City Ambitions Daily leaderboard, we store the name you type, your four choices for that day (stock and price for each product), the resulting cash, the page language and a random player ID kept in your browser. The name and score are shown publicly to every visitor. They are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Use a nickname, not your real name. Nothing is sent unless you press the button; to have an entry removed, write to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all local game data. To have analytics data linked to your app instance, or a feedback message, deleted, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will appear on this page.
