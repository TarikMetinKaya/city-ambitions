Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. What City Ambitions and cityambitions.com collect, and why.

City Ambitions is published by TMK Tech (Tarik Metin Kaya). This policy covers the iPhone game and this website.

The game

Your saved game

Your company and progress are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes that save.

Anonymous app user ID

The first time you open the game it creates a random app user ID. It contains no name, email address or other detail about you. We use it to link your analytics, crash reports, purchases and messages to the same installation of the game.

Analytics and crash reports

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to count a small set of gameplay events (for example finishing the opening, a daily close or placing an order) with small numeric details such as the game day, and Firebase Crashlytics to send us a report when the app crashes (the state of the app at that moment). Both record basic device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, country) and are linked to the anonymous app user ID. We use this only to see where players get stuck, fix crashes and improve the game. See how Google handles Firebase data.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

Players who have not bought the Full Game may see full-screen ads between days (interstitial ads), served by Unity Ads. Ads only start after the first week of play. Buying the Full Game or the Supporter Pack removes ads for good.

To serve and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect: a device identifier (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking), ad interaction data (which ads were shown and whether you tapped them), usage data, purchase history, coarse location (country level), performance data, and device information (model, language, screen, connection type). Unity handles this data under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission

When you open the game, iOS asks whether City Ambitions may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, ads may be personalised using your advertising identifier, and Firebase may read the same identifier so we can measure which of our own ad campaigns brought players to the game. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is never read and the ads you see are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

The Full Game and the Supporter Pack are one-time in-app purchases handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchases are verified through Apple and through RevenueCat, a purchase service that receives the anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

With your permission the game schedules local notifications on your device, for example a reminder that your shop is waiting. They are created on the device and no notification data is sent to us.

Feedback and messages with the developer

If you choose to send us a note from the game, we receive the text you typed together with the game day, the moment it was asked, the app version, your device model, your language and region, and the anonymous app user ID. The developer may answer: the reply appears in the game, and you can write back. Notes, replies and your answers are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to the anonymous app user ID, and read only to answer you, fix problems and improve the game. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

What the game does not collect

No accounts, and no names, email addresses, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell your data.

This website

cityambitions.com uses Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) to count visits and clicks: pages viewed, the steps and result of the browser demo, taps on the App Store button, language changes and opened questions. Google receives your IP address (shortened for visitors in the EU), device and browser type, approximate location, the page you came from and a random cookie ID. Visitors in Europe are asked first and the script only loads after they allow it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); elsewhere it loads by default. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. The data is kept for at most 14 months, is not used for advertising and is not sold. Fonts and images are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes the technical data every web request carries, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

The browser demo runs in your browser; its steps are counted only as described above. Your browser's local storage remembers your cookie choice and whether you dismissed the language suggestion; that never leaves your device.

Daily leaderboard

If you choose to add your score to the City Ambitions Daily leaderboard, we store the name you type, your four choices for that day (stock and price for each product), the resulting cash, the page language and a random player ID kept in your browser. The name and score are shown publicly to every visitor. They are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Use a nickname, not your real name. Nothing is sent unless you press the button; to have an entry removed, write to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data on your device. To have the analytics data, crash reports, notes and messages linked to your app user ID deleted, write to tmkdevcomp@gmail.com. Telling us roughly when you played and your device model helps us find them. For data held by Unity Ads, Unity's privacy policy above explains how to make a request to Unity.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will appear on this page.
