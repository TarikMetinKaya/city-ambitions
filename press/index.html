About the game

City Ambitions is a business simulation for iPhone. You inherit your mother's debt-ridden shop in New York and have 180 days to pay back $100,000 by running stores: setting every price, ordering stock, staffing shifts and reading the books each night.

There is no walking and no waiting on energy timers. Every screen is a decision with a visible result. Demand depends on price, variety, reputation, opening hours, checkout capacity and the regulars a shop earns over time. Later the city opens up: storage, delivery routes to branches, imported containers, a factory, property and rival stores on your block. The game is free to download, the first shop has no time limit, and a single purchase unlocks the rest.
