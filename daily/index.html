Daily puzzle

One stall. One price. Everyone gets the same day.

Every day there is a new product and a new street of shoppers, the same for every player in the world. Choose how many to stock and what to charge, then see how close you got to the best possible profit.

How it works

You start with $1,000 and sell two products in a row. Each shopper has a price they will not go above and wants one to three items. Stock too few and you sell out; stock too many and the leftovers are given away at closing; price too high and people walk on. The first product's profit is added to your cash before you buy the second. Your score is your gain as a share of the best gain anyone could have made today. One try per day.
