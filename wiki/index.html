Staff and wages

  • A day has four blocks of 6 hours: morning, afternoon, evening and night. Shoppers arrive 20% / 35% / 35% / 10% across them.
  • One employee serves about 26 customers in a block.
  • Hourly wages run from $14 to $18, so one staffed block costs $84 to $108.
  • An employee works up to 42 hours a week (7 blocks). Hours beyond that cost 50% more.
  • A raise is $2 an hour. Morale starts at 80; below 35 an employee is unhappy.
  • Daily wages for other roles: warehouse hand $90, driver $80, cook $150, factory worker $60, regional manager $514.

Factory

  • The factory opens at a company value of $50,000.
  • Raw materials for one unit cost 28% of that product's catalog cost.
  • A factory worker costs $60 a day; how many units a worker makes in a morning depends on the product.
  • The workshop runs 1 line; the factory floor runs 3.
  • Surplus the stores cannot use sells wholesale at about 60% of catalog cost.

Fast food rush

  • The kitchen cooks 28 units in one rush. Two upgrades raise that to 44 and 60, for $1,500 and $3,500.
  • Each cook adds 14 units a rush and costs $150 a day; up to 2 cooks.

Delivery contracts

  • A contracted line costs 15% more than ordering the same units by hand.
  • Up to 12 product lines across the company.
  • Contracts open after 7 Daily Closes.

The goal

The family debt is $100,000, due on Day 180. The game compares it with your company value: at 100% the debt is cleared, at 60% or more the bank takes the rest in instalments.
