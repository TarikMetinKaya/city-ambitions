Factory tycoon game on iPhone: make it yourself or buy it wholesale

Most factory games are about conveyor belts. In City Ambitions the factory is a business decision: is it cheaper to make this product yourself, or to keep buying it wholesale?

Updated 2026-11-12

How the factory works

From version 1.2, City Ambitions' factory runs on recipes. Each recipe turns raw materials into a product on a machine, with workers on the floor. You buy the raw materials from a wholesaler or import them by container, the machines produce every morning, and the goods go straight to your stores' storage.

The make-or-buy decision

The factory screen shows, for every product, what it costs to make and what it costs to buy. That gap is the whole game:

  • Make it when the gap is large and your stores sell enough to keep the line busy.
  • Buy it when demand is small or uneven: an idle line still costs rent and wages.
  • Sell the surplus wholesale when the factory makes more than your stores can sell.

Getting started

  1. Grow one or two stores until you know what sells every day.
  2. Start with a small floor and one recipe for your best-selling product.
  3. Keep raw materials stocked: an empty input stops the line.
  4. Add machines and workers only when the line is full every morning.
  5. Compare the factory's cost per unit with your old wholesale price in the Daily Close.

The rest of the company

The factory is one part of a bigger business sim: shops, supermarkets and fast food in New York neighborhoods, branches with delivery routes and three kinds of vehicle, container imports, delivery contracts, bank loans and, when you are rich, a luxury life. See how to run a store in a business sim for the basics, and the best business sim games on iPhone for other options.
