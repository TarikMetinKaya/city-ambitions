The best business sim games on iPhone (2026)

Looking for a real business simulator on your phone? Here is what is out there in 2026, what each game is actually about, and how to choose.

Updated 2026-11-12

Full disclosure: we make City Ambitions, the first game on this list. Every other game here is made by someone else and is listed because it is worth knowing about.

What makes a good business sim (and not just an idle clicker)

Many games in the App Store's tycoon section are idle games: numbers go up while you wait, and the main decision is which upgrade to tap. A business simulator is different. Look for:

  • Prices you set yourself, with demand that reacts to them.
  • Costs that can sink you: rent, wages, stock that does not sell.
  • Staff and opening hours that limit how much you can sell.
  • A way to grow: more stores, better suppliers, your own production.
  • Few or no timers and ads standing between you and your next decision.

The list

1. City Ambitions: a deep shop-and-company sim for iPhone

A single-player business simulator set in New York. You inherit your mother's shop and $100,000 of family debt, and you have 180 days to clear it. You set every price, order every box, staff four six-hour shifts and read a full profit and loss every night. Later you open branches in other neighborhoods, rent storage, run delivery routes, import containers, build a factory and face rival stores on your block.

  • Best for: players who want real numbers and real decisions on a phone.
  • Model: free to start; one purchase unlocks the full game. No energy timers.
  • Platforms: iPhone (also Apple silicon Mac and Apple Vision Pro). Not on Android yet.
  • Try it: play the first day in your browser, or get it on the App Store.

2. Sim Companies: an online economy with other players

A multiplayer business game where you build production chains and trade on a market made of other real players. Prices come from them, not from a script. It is closer to an economic strategy game than to running a single shop, and it rewards patience over weeks.

  • Best for: players who like markets, supply chains and competing with real people.
  • Platforms: iPhone, Android and web.

3. Game Dev Tycoon: run a game studio

You start in a garage making games and grow into a studio, choosing topics, genres and platforms and reading reviews of what you ship. A small, focused management game with a clear goal and a strong sense of progress.

  • Best for: players who want a contained story of building a company from nothing.
  • Platforms: iPhone, Android and PC.

4. Good Pizza, Great Pizza: a small shop, one customer at a time

A pizza shop where you read each customer's order, make the pizza and earn enough to buy better equipment. Lighter than a full business sim, but it captures the feeling of running a shop day by day.

  • Best for: short, relaxed sessions with a shop at the center.
  • Platforms: iPhone, Android and PC.

5. Business Inc. and Business Empire: big-company fantasies

Both are popular tycoon games about growing a fortune across many kinds of businesses. They lean toward the empire-building fantasy and fast progress rather than the details of one store's pricing and staffing.

  • Best for: players who want scale and quick growth.
  • Platforms: iPhone and Android.

6. Founder Sim: a startup instead of a shop

A newer iPhone game (2026) about founding and running a startup. If your interest is the company side, such as hiring, funding and product, rather than retail, it is worth a look.

  • Platforms: iPhone.

Questions

What is the most realistic business simulator on iPhone?

It depends on what you mean by realistic. For running a store, with prices, demand, wages, rent, tax and stock, City Ambitions models each of those directly. For a market made of real players, Sim Companies is the deepest.

Are there business sim games without heavy ads?

Yes. Look for games sold as a one-time purchase, and check the App Store page before you download: idle games that ask you to watch an ad to double earnings are a different kind of game. City Ambitions is free to start and one purchase unlocks the full game. See offline business tycoon games for what to look for.

Is there a business sim like this for Android?

Sim Companies and Game Dev Tycoon are on Android; City Ambitions is iPhone-only for now, but its first day runs in any phone's browser.
