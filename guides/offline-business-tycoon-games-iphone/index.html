Offline business tycoon games for iPhone, and how to spot the ad-heavy ones

Plenty of tycoon games need a connection to show you ads, sync timers or check a server. Here is how to find business games that play anywhere, and how to read an App Store page before you download.

Updated 2026-11-12

What "offline" really means for a mobile game

A game plays offline when everything you do is calculated and saved on your phone. Signs that it does:

  • No account or sign-in to start playing.
  • Saves on the device, not "in the cloud only".
  • No server-driven events that need you online at a certain hour.

Buying, restoring a purchase or downloading an update always needs a connection, on any game.

How to spot an ad-heavy tycoon game before you install it

Many games in the App Store's tycoon section are idle games: numbers go up on their own, and the main way to go faster is to watch an ad. They can be fun, but they are a different kind of game. Clues on the App Store page:

  • Screenshots showing "x2" or "watch to boost" buttons.
  • Energy, stamina or "come back in 4 hours" timers.
  • A long list of small in-app purchases (gem packs, coin bundles) instead of a single unlock.
  • Reviews mentioning ads after every action.

A business simulator, by contrast, makes you decide prices, stock and staff, and a bad decision costs you money.

City Ambitions

City Ambitions is a single-player business simulator set in New York. Your company is saved on your iPhone, there is no account, and the simulation runs on the device, so you can play without a connection once it is installed. There are no energy timers and no automatic money: you set prices, order stock, staff shifts and read a full profit and loss every night, then expand into supermarkets, fast food, branches, containers and your own factory.

Price: free to start; one in-app purchase unlocks the full game. Check the App Store page for the current details.
