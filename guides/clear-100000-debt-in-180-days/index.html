How to clear $100,000 of debt in 180 days in City Ambitions

Your mother's shop owes $100,000 and the bank wants it in 180 days. The good news: the game does not ask for $100,000 in cash. Here is what it measures, and a plan to get there.

Updated 2026-11-12

What the game checks on Day 180

When Day 180 closes, City Ambitions compares your company value with the $100,000 debt. Company value is everything the company owns, minus what it still owes:

  • Counts: cash, stock on shelves and in storage (at cost), branches (part of what you spent setting them up), buildings you own, deposits, shares.
  • Comes off: container orders not yet paid for, and what you still owe the bank on a loan.
  • Does not count: money spent on the luxury life. It is spending, and the cash is gone.

There are three endings, and none of them is game over:

Company value on Day 180What happens
$100,000 or moreThe debt is paid. The shop is your mother's again.
$60,000 to $99,999Short, but close: the bank takes the rest in instalments and the doors stay open.
Below $60,000The shop goes back to the bank. Everything else you built is still yours, and you keep playing.

The Home screen shows your progress against the debt every day, so you always know where you stand.

Days 1 to 7: learn one shop properly

You start with $2,000 and one storefront. The first week is not about money; it is about learning what your shop's numbers mean.

  • Sell out your umbrellas on Day 1 at a price above market: the rain makes people pay. See getting started.
  • Never let the shelf run empty. Shoppers who find nothing leave, and regulars stop coming.
  • Read the Daily Close every night. Revenue minus cost of goods, wages, rent and tax is your real profit.

Days 8 to 40: make the first shop boring

A shop that runs itself is the base you build on. Aim for a full shelf, a price near the top of what shoppers accept, and staff on the busy blocks.

  • Price up until profit stops rising. Change one price at a time and compare the next close. See how pricing works.
  • Open the hours that pay. A block with few shoppers can cost more in wages than it earns. See staff and opening hours.
  • Earn regulars. They come back every day without being won over again. See regulars and Store Pulse.

Days 40 to 120: grow the value, not only the cash

This is where most of the $100,000 comes from. Profit that sits in the bank counts, but so does profit you turn into things the company owns.

  1. Open a second store in another neighborhood once the first one is full every day. Each branch adds its own daily profit.
  2. Cut your unit cost. Storage and container imports lower what each product costs you; the saving is profit on every sale. See stock, storage and containers.
  3. Try a supermarket or fast food (version 1.2) when you can staff it. More shoppers, smaller margins.
  4. Consider a factory for a product you sell in volume. See the factory guide.

Days 120 to 180: protect what you have

  • Watch the progress bar, not the cash. A big container order lowers company value until it is paid and landed; time large orders so they arrive and sell before Day 180.
  • Hold off on the luxury life until the debt is covered. A watch or a yacht is a reward, not an investment.
  • Use a bank loan for growth only. The loan's cash counts and what you owe comes off, so a loan only helps if the business you build with it earns more than the interest.

Mistakes that leave you short

  • Expanding before the first shop is profitable: two weak stores lose money twice as fast.
  • Pricing far below market to look busy when staff cannot serve the crowd.
  • Over-ordering perishable goods in a supermarket: spoiled stock is money thrown away.
  • Spending on luxury before Day 180.

Questions

Do I need $100,000 in cash on Day 180?

No. City Ambitions measures company value: cash plus stock, branches, buildings, deposits and shares, minus unpaid orders and loans. Luxury purchases do not count.

What happens if I do not clear the debt?

Above 60% of the debt, the bank takes the rest in instalments and the shop stays open. Below that, the shop goes back to the bank, but the rest of your company is still yours and the game goes on.

Does the game end on Day 180?

No. The deadline settles the debt once, and then you keep playing with no time limit.

Can I try the start before downloading?

Yes: the first day plays in your browser.
