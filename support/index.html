Support

Help with City Ambitions on iPhone.

Contact

Email tmkdevcomp@gmail.com. For a bug, include the app version shown on the App Store page, your iPhone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps that make it happen. A screenshot or screen recording helps. Please do not send personal information.

Purchases

What does the Full Game unlock?

You can run your first shop for as long as you like. The Full Game is a one-time purchase that opens the rest of the city: more storefronts, storage, containers, your own factory, buildings, deposits and shares.

I bought the Full Game but it is locked

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase. This also works after reinstalling or on a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your save

Where is my company saved?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes the company.

How do I start over?

Delete and reinstall the app. Your Full Game purchase is kept: restore it from Settings.

Playing

What is the goal?

Build the company and clear the $100,000 family debt by Day 180. The company can continue afterward.

Does time continue when I close the game?

Only businesses covered by Executive Autopilot progress while you are away.

Why did sales stop?

Open Store Pulse. It shows what is holding the shop back: demand, stock, staff, checkout capacity, price and opening hours. The Store Pulse guide explains each one.

When do orders arrive?

Local wholesale orders arrive the next morning. Other suppliers and containers take longer.

What are regulars?

Customers earned through reliable service. They come back on their own and become demand you built.

Why rent storage?

Storage holds stock that does not fit on the shelf and refills the shelf each morning. Its rent is charged at every close.

What can happen to a container?

Source, size, payment, travel time, customs, delays and damage can all change its cost and arrival.

More help

The guides explain prices, regulars, stock and staff with the numbers the game uses.
